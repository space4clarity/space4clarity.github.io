About

A place to keep the thread.

This site is a small personal blog for essays, notes, observations, and unfinished thoughts that still feel useful.

The design is intentionally quiet: dark background, readable type, simple navigation, and enough space for the writing to breathe.

Daily Reading is an automated literary series: one passage from a Project Gutenberg book, one focused reflection, and one question. Entries are generated and checked by AI, with quotations verified against downloaded source texts. They are interpretations rather than personal accounts by Jesse, and automated checks can still miss mistakes.

Written and maintained by Jesse Scribbler.
